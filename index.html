How to play

  • Tap a tile to turn it clockwise. Right-click, or long-press on a phone, turns it back.
  • Carry light from the glowing core to every lantern.
  • The level ends when every tile is lit. No loops, no loose ends.
  • Finish within par moves for three stars.
  • You get 3 undos per level, so think before you tap.
  • Keys: arrows move, Space rotates, Z undoes, R restarts.
